# Bom Generation

Use after a hardware-neutral specification exists. Call `generate_bom_tool`; call `compare_bom_options_tool` for alternatives. Preserve the requirement and specification link for every line. Validate quantity, compatibility, lifecycle, dated price, availability, origin evidence and support. Leave manufacturer, SKU, price and origin unknown until evidenced; never substitute silently. Route restricted projects through sourcing and compliance review. A human procurement approver selects products. Output JSON/CSV, evidence gaps and comparisons.
