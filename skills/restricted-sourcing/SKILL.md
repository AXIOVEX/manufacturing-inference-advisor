# Restricted Sourcing

Use for CMMC, ITAR, export control, contract clauses or enterprise restrictions. Obtain an authorized project policy and call `screen_bom_for_compliance_tool`. Preserve origin documents, dates, reviewers, supplier status, firmware and driver provenance. Unknown origin is `needs_evidence`; brand headquarters and marketing do not prove eligibility. Quarantine prohibited or unresolved lines without inventing replacements. A qualified human approves policy and procurement. Output line decisions, failed rules, evidence gaps and review status.
