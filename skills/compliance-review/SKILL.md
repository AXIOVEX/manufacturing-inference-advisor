# Compliance Review

Use after restricted sourcing or when controlled data appears. Call `screen_bom_for_compliance_tool` with an organization-approved policy. Review data flows, clauses, ownership, firmware/software provenance, cloud dependencies, support access and personnel constraints as well as origin. Results are workflow screening, not legal certification. Missing evidence blocks automated approval. Qualified legal, export-control, CMMC and procurement reviewers decide. Output findings, evidence, questions, limitations and status.
