# Deployment Planning

Use after architecture, specification and sourcing review. Call `generate_deployment_plan_tool`. Preserve approved versions and dependencies. Cover site readiness, segmentation, OS/firmware, runtime, serving, agents, identity, secrets, telemetry, backup, recovery, acceptance and rollback. Stop when prerequisite evidence is missing. Do not authorize procurement or cutover. Require human approval for security, regulated purchases and production. Output phases, owners to assign, exit evidence and rollback checkpoints.
